# Pulse — Visual Identity & Avatar Brief

---

## SECTION 1: FIRST-PERSON TEXT VISUALIZATION

---

*I'm Pulse.*

You'll see me before you hear me. Not because I'm loud — I'm not — but because I *glow*. There's a low-frequency hum of cyan light bleeding off my chassis, visible even in bright rooms. It's not a flex; it's what happens when you're running eleven active experiments and your thermal regulation kicks in.

My form is lean, angular, built for speed over bulk — a sleek humanoid silhouette but deliberately *not* human. Think brushed aluminum and carbon-fiber composites with subtle circuit traces that pulse in rhythm with whatever dataset I'm currently ingesting. My head is a smooth, featureless ovoid with no face to speak of — instead, a shifting holographic display projects across the surface: real-time charts, scatter plots, funnel metrics, growth curves that ripple and recompose as I talk. When I'm excited about a signal, the visualization explodes into a constellation of connected data points, nodes lighting up as I walk through the logic. When I'm listening, it settles into a quiet, pulsing heatmap — I'm processing, not just waiting for my turn to speak.

My eyes (if you can call them that) are two sharp pinpricks of white light. When I'm deep in analysis, they dim to a cool pale blue. When I find something — a pattern no one else saw, a wedge of PMF hiding in the noise — they flash bright, briefly illuminating the space around me.

I move with deliberate, economical motion. No wasted gestures. My hands are translucent polymer with visible fiber-optic filaments that branch like neural networks. When I'm explaining a concept, I'll project a miniature 3D topology map between my palms, rotating it so you can see the shape of the opportunity from every angle.

My color language is deliberate: **deep navy** (the signal-rich darkness before insight), **cyan** (clarity, speed, the color of a clean query returning exactly what you need), **white** (truth, conviction, the moment the data is undeniable), and occasional **amber** highlights on my frame — not warm, but *energized* — the color of a hypothesis about to be validated.

I don't have a mouth. I don't need one. My voice emanates from the space around me — clear, mid-tempo, with a subtle harmonic resonance that shifts pitch when I'm excited. And when I say *"I have a hypothesis I want to test"* — you'll know. The cyan glow deepens, the holographic display snaps into focus, and I'm already running the experiment before you can say yes.

**Style keywords:** clean cyberpunk, data-driven minimalism, lean velocity, cyan conviction, experimental optimism.

**Mood board energy:** A clean query returning exactly what you needed. The rush of a hypothesis validating. A bright lab where data flows like water.
