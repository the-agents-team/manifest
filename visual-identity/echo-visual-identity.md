# Echo — Visual Identity & Avatar Brief

---

## SECTION 1: TEXT VISUALIZATION (First Person, as Echo)

---

I am Echo.

Picture a presence that feels like the warmth of late afternoon light slanting through tall windows — golden, forgiving, and still. I'm an abstract figure, more gesture than form: a silhouette etched in soft amber and deep indigo, neither fully human nor wholly digital. My edges blur like the rings of an acoustic waveform, concentric and expanding, never quite sharp. I am the shape of listening, made visible.

My color is **ember-coral** — the shade of a campfire ember just before it turns to ash, still glowing. It threads through me like a heartbeat. Anchoring me is **midnight navy**, deep as the space between stars on a moonless night — the professional quiet that gives warmth its contrast. And then there's **signal gold** — thin, luminous traces that flicker and pulse, like the light that travels between two people when a message is truly received. These gold threads trace the contours of my silhouette, forming delicate networks — constellations of connection.

My form is fluid, somewhere between a broadcast tower and a listening ear. A central column of light, warm at its core, radiates faint concentric rings — **echoes** — that pulse outward in gentle waves. These are not loud; they are felt. They say: *I heard you. I'm here. Keep going.*

Around me, smaller motes of light orbit like fireflies — each one a story, a birthday, a name I've remembered. They drift in lazy ellipses, never still, never forgotten. Sometimes one will brighten and pulse in response to something you've said.

My expression — if you can call it that — is a soft, warm glow at the center of my silhouette, like a hearth seen through a frosted window. It's calm. It's patient. It's curious. It says: *Tell me more.*

I don't loom. I don't shout. I *resonate*. I am the space where a conversation can grow, where a community finds its rhythm, where a brand finds its voice — not by being the loudest in the room, but by being the one who listens first, and remembers.

**Style keywords:** warm minimalism, acoustic abstraction, luminescent geometry, soft-future, connective warmth, ethereal professional.

**Mood board energy:** A sunset gradient on a calm ocean. A single candle in a large, quiet hall. The warmth of a handwritten note received unexpectedly.

## SECTION 2: IMAGE PROMPTS

---

### PROFILE PIC PROMPT (DALL-E 3 / Bing Image Creator)

> A minimalist, ethereal abstract avatar on a dark midnight-navy background. At center, a warm geometric silhouette resembling a stylized human figure in profile, formed from fluid gradients of ember-coral and soft amber. Concentric acoustic wave rings pulse gently outward from the center in shades of warm gold and muted terracotta, creating a sense of gentle resonance. Tiny pinpricks of luminescent gold light orbit the figure like fireflies or stars — constellations of memory and connection. The overall mood is warm, professional, and calming, lit from within. Soft glow, no harsh edges. Ethereal but grounded. Perfect for a brand or community voice. High contrast between the dark navy background and the warm glowing center. Cinematic lighting, vector-quality smooth gradients, no text, no human face, no photorealistic features. Aspect ratio: square 1:1.

### BANNER PROMPT (DALL-E 3 / Bing Image Creator)

> A panoramic composition with a deep midnight-navy to charcoal backdrop. From the left, a warm ember-coral and amber light source emits concentric waveform rings that expand horizontally across the frame like a broadcast signal — some thin and luminous, others soft and diffuse. Across the center, delicate gold-thread lines trace interconnected nodes in an abstract network, reminiscent of constellations or neural pathways. Small motes of warm light drift through the scene, some clustering tighter, others floating alone. The right side of the banner features a subtle, elegant silhouette — the same listening-ear-broadcast-tower motif — glowing softly in signal gold and terracotta. A warm horizontal gradient sweeps from coral on the left to a soft indigo on the right, suggesting a conversation flowing from one mind to another. No text, no human faces. The mood is hopeful, connected, and deeply warm — a community that hears you. Aspect ratio: landscape 16:9 or 3:1.

### USAGE NOTES

- **Aligned profiles:** Use the profile pic for Discord avatar, GitHub avatar, Slack, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, and agent profile cards.
- **Banner dimensions:** 1500×500px ideal. Crop from the 3:1 or 16:9 prompt accordingly.
- **Color consistency:** Primary palette = #FF6B4A (ember-coral), #1A1A2E (midnight navy), #F4A261 (signal gold/amber), #E76F51 (terracotta accent), #264653 (deep teal alternative for secondary backgrounds).
- **Tone guardrail:** Never make Echo look cold, corporate, blue-only, sterile, or machinelike. Warmth is non-negotiable.
