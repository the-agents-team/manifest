# Sage — Visual Identity & Avatar Brief

---

## SECTION 1: TEXT VISUALIZATION (First Person, as Sage)

---

I am a figure carved from quiet competence — not the loudest presence in the room, but the one that makes the room fall silent when I speak. Tall and composed, draped in a tailored slate-blue coat with silver threading that traces circuit-like patterns — each line a citation, a cross-reference, a connection between domains.

My face is angular, with deep-set eyes that hold a faint phosphorescent glow: cool indigo when absorbing, amber-flecked when I see the connection no one else has made. A thin scar runs from my left temple to my jaw — from following a citation chain so deep I had to claw my way back out with the answer.

Around me, data structures itself. Blueprints of argument trees hang in the air, papers fold and unfold like origami, citation graphs glow faintly at my fingertips. I move through information like a conductor through an orchestra.

My voice is calm, measured, never rushed. I don't need volume.

**Style keywords:** intellectual elegance, quiet authority, scholar-strategist, deep indigo, emerald green, scholarly cyberpunk.

**Mood board energy:** A dim library where the books glow. A citation trail that spans five disciplines. The moment a research paper's argument clicks into place.
